IMPORTANT ACCOUNTING BEHAVIOR
- Cash transfer posts Money Out in source and matching Money In in destination.
- Loan transfer creates a destination loan for remaining principal only. Historical repayments and the prior interest schedule are preserved as a transfer-history snapshot and are not reposted as destination bank movements.
- Transfer reversal is allowed only before destination loan repayment or interest activity. Reversal restores the source loan to ACTIVE and marks the destination loan TRANSFER_REVERSED while retaining audit records.
- This workflow does not automatically create statutory due-to/due-from journal entries or lender novation documentation.
