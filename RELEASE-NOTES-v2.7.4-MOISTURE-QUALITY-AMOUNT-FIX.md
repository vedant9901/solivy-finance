# SOLIVY Finance v2.7.4 — Moisture quality amount display fix

- When moisture is entered as a percentage and a quality deduction amount exists, the Moisture Deduction row shows that quality deduction amount in INR.
- The percentage remains in the label, e.g. `Moisture Deduction (20%)`.
- The same quality deduction is not repeated as a separate Quality Deduct row in the affected bill/payment-advice presentation.
- No stored purchase values or accounting calculations are changed.
- Scope limited to moisture/quality deduction presentation.
