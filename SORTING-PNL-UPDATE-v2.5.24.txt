SOLIVY Finance v2.5.24 — Sorting and Purchase MIS P&L update

Changes limited to app/page.tsx:
- Detailed MIS module tables now provide sortable headers for every displayed column.
- Existing shared Table component already supports sorting every column for modules that use it, on desktop and mobile.
- MIS Excel exports are sorted oldest-to-newest by the detected date/month field regardless of the on-screen sort direction.
- Purchase & Rate Analysis now shows a management P&L summary strip (purchase net payable, trading gross profit, estimated financing income, estimated net profit/loss).
- No purchase-level profit allocation is fabricated; purchase lots are not linked to sales lots in the current data model. The P&L strip is a filtered-period management estimate, not statutory accounts.
