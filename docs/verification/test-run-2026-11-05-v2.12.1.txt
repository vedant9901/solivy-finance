## NOT VERIFIED — stated plainly

  implemented. purchaseCalc has no threshold term. If a rate applies above 14%, it must be supplied
  and will be added as a company setting. Section 7 of PAYMENT-ADVICE-MOISTURE-v2.12.1.md sets out
  the decision needed.
- Whether a moisture reading should ever deduct by itself: the legacy branch of purchaseCalc deducts
